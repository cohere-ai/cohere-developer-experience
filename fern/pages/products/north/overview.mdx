North brings together LLMs, search, and automation into one secure AI workspace.
Build custom agents, automate multi-step workflows, and connect to your enterprise
data and tools — all within your own environment.

<Note>
This is a prototype page demonstrating the two-level tab navigation (product tabs on top,
the product's own tabs below).
</Note>

## What you can do with North

- **Chat** with your connected data sources for grounded, cited answers.
- **Automations** that chain specialized agents into multi-step workflows.
- **Tools & connectors** for Gmail, Google Drive, Jira, SharePoint, MCP servers, and more.

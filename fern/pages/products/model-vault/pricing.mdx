| Embed 4 | Small | $4.00 | $2,500 | $25,000 |
| Embed 4 | Medium | $5.00 | $3,250 | $32,500 |
| Rerank 3.5 | Medium | $5.00 | $3,250 | $32,500 |
| Rerank 4 Pro | Large | $10.00 | $6,500 | $65,000 |

Text watermarking embeds a statistical signal in Cohere's generated text. It helps meet EU AI Act
transparency obligations for AI-generated text.

## Availability

Model Vault vaults in the EU region serve watermarked flavors of supported text models.

<Note>
Deploy your EU vault normally. API calls and responses are unchanged; generated text carries the
watermarked signal.
</Note>

For regional availability, see [Model Vault regions](../docs/model-vault/regions).

## Verification

Watermarked output from EU Model Vault deployments can later be verified with Cohere-approved
detection. Verification is arranged with Cohere; contact Cohere for details.

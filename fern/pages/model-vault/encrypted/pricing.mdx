Model Vault Encrypted is priced separately from [Standard Vault](/docs/model-vault/standard/pricing)
because it runs on confidential-computing hardware with additional protections such as remote
attestation and end-to-end encryption.

## During the beta

Model Vault Encrypted is currently in beta and is offered free of charge to design partners. If you
would like to evaluate it, [contact Cohere](https://cohere.com/contact-sales) to join the program.

## General availability

Full pricing details for Model Vault Encrypted will be shared as the product matures. In the
meantime, [contact Cohere](https://cohere.com/contact-sales) for current rates and to discuss your
requirements.

For the pricing of the non-encrypted product, see
[Standard Vault Pricing](/docs/model-vault/standard/pricing).

Model Vault (Encrypted AI) is Cohere's offering for running models in fully encrypted,
privacy-preserving environments. It lets you deploy and serve Cohere models so that your
data and the model weights remain protected end to end.

<Note title="Work in progress">
  This documentation is an early draft. Sections and content will be expanded over the
  coming iterations.
</Note>

## What you can do with Model Vault

- Deploy Cohere models into encrypted, isolated environments.
- Keep prompts, responses, and model weights protected end to end.
- Integrate Model Vault into your existing Cohere workflows.

Today, Cohere Labs, Cohere’s research arm, is proud to announce 
[Aya Vision](https://cohere.com/blog/aya-vision), a state-of-the-art multimodal large language model 
excelling across multiple languages and modalities. Aya Vision outperforms the leading open-weight models 
in critical benchmarks for language, text, and image capabilities. 

## Technical Details

Built as a foundation for multilingual and multimodal communication, this groundbreaking AI model supports 
tasks such as image captioning, visual question answering, text generation, and translations from both 
texts and images into coherent text.

Refer to [Aya Vision](/docs/aya-multimodal) for more information.
